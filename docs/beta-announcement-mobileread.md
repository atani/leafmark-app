---

Hi everyone,

Like many of you I never really got over Marvin. I tried the alternatives —
Apple Books locks my highlights in, the subscription apps want $100/year for
my own books — and eventually decided to build the thing I was missing.

**Leafmark** is an EPUB reader for iPhone and iPad with one principle:
your books and highlights are yours.

What works today:

- Library with cover grid; import from Files, share sheet, or drop files in
  via Finder/iTunes file sharing
- Highlights in 4 colors + notes, with **Markdown export** (works great with
  Obsidian/Notion)
- Full-text search
- Dictionary look-up from the selection menu
- Reading stats: time per day/week, streak, per-book totals
- 3 themes, 5 fonts, line height / margins / scroll mode, and two-column
  layout on iPad landscape
- No account, no tracking, no cloud — everything stays on your device.
  One-time purchase when it ships, never a subscription

What it is not (yet): no collections/tags, no sync, no PDF, no DRM.

I am looking for a small group of beta testers via TestFlight — especially
former Marvin users who can tell me which missing feature would actually stop
you from switching. Reply here or DM me and I will send a TestFlight link
when the build is up.

Screenshots attached. Happy to answer anything.
