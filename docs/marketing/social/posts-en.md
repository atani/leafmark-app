## Posting order

Post twice per week for three weeks. Use the same theme on Pinterest and Bluesky 2–3 days apart so results remain attributable by channel.

1. Week 1: Own your highlights
2. Week 2: Buy once
3. Week 3: Private by design

Record impressions, outbound clicks, App Store product-page views, downloads, and Pro purchases by campaign token. Do not change both the creative and copy during this first six-post run.

## Accuracy notes

- Do not claim “no cloud.” Leafmark offers optional sync through the user's own iCloud.
- Do not put a fixed US price on shared US/Canada artwork; App Store pricing differs by storefront.
- The artwork uses real current app screenshots. Do not substitute generated app UI.
