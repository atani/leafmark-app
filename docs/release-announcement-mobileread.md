---

Hi everyone,

Like many of you I never really got over Marvin. I tried the alternatives —
Apple Books locks my highlights in, the subscription apps want $100/year for
my own books — and eventually decided to build the thing I was missing.

**Leafmark** is now on the App Store. It is an EPUB reader for iPhone and
iPad with one principle: your books and highlights are yours.

What it does today:

- Library with cover grid; import from Files (iCloud Drive / Google Drive /
  Dropbox), the share sheet, or Finder file sharing
- Highlights in 4 colors + notes, with **Markdown export** (works great with
  Obsidian/Notion)
- Full-text search and dictionary look-up from the selection menu
- Reading stats: time per day/week, streak, per-book totals
- 3 themes, adjustable fonts, line height / margins / scroll mode, and
  two-column layout on iPad landscape
- No account, no tracking, no cloud — everything stays on your device

It ships with three public-domain classics (Frankenstein, Meditations, and
The Autobiography of Benjamin Franklin) so you can judge the rendering and
the highlighting feel before importing your own library.

Reading is free with no limits — import everything and try the feel. A
one-time purchase (no subscription, ever) unlocks unlimited highlights,
Markdown export and reading statistics.

What it is not (yet): no collections/tags, no sync, no PDF, no DRM.

I would especially appreciate feedback from former Marvin users: which
missing feature would actually stop you from switching? That list is what I
build next.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6781045740

Screenshots attached. Happy to answer anything.
