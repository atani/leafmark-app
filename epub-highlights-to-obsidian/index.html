From an EPUB to a note in your vault

  1. Import a DRM-free EPUB into Leafmark. Use the Files picker to choose a book on your device or in an available Files provider, such as iCloud Drive. PDF and DRM-protected books are not supported.
  2. Highlight a passage and add a note. Select text while reading, choose a highlight color, and add your thoughts. The free plan allows up to three highlights per book.
  3. Open Highlights and export your reading notes. Preview the Markdown, choose reading order or grouping by highlight color, then export. Save the file using the iOS share sheet.
  4. Add the .md file to your Obsidian vault. Move or copy it into your vault folder using Files if that folder is accessible there, or transfer it to the device where your vault lives. Open Obsidian and find the note in that folder. Available share targets depend on your installed apps and vault setup.

Export is a snapshot of your notes at that moment. Later edits in Leafmark do not automatically update the file in Obsidian. Keep or rename earlier exports before saving another file with the same name.

What the Markdown looks like

This illustrative example follows Leafmark’s reading-order export format. The quoted sentence is from Mary Shelley’s Frankenstein; the note below it is an example reader note.

---
title: "Frankenstein"
author: "Mary Shelley"
source: Leafmark
exported: 2026-11-15
---

# Frankenstein
by Mary Shelley

> Nothing is so painful to the human mind as a great and sudden change.

Example reader note: connect this passage to my notes on change.

The book metadata becomes note properties. Highlights remain blockquotes, with your notes below them. You can edit the file with any Markdown editor.

Before you start

Can I export Kindle or Apple Books highlights?

This workflow starts with a DRM-free EPUB imported into Leafmark. It does not import annotations from Kindle, Apple Books or other readers, and does not remove DRM.

Does Leafmark sync directly with Obsidian?

No. Export a Markdown file and add it to your vault yourself. Leafmark’s iCloud sync keeps its own highlights, bookmarks and reading positions in step between your devices; it does not update an Obsidian vault.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No Leafmark account is needed. Reading is free. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited annotation and export. Your choice of Obsidian vault synchronization is separate.

Are my books or highlights sent to Leafmark?

Your reading content stays on your device or in your own iCloud. From version 1.20, Firebase Analytics measures anonymous app and purchase-funnel activity, and RevenueCat processes purchases. Read the privacy policy for details.
