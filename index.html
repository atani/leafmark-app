Leafmark

Your highlights are yours.

A buy-once EPUB reader for people who read closely and take their notes with them. No subscription, no account, no cloud lock-in.

Features

  • Reflowable EPUB rendering with light, sepia and dark themes
  • Highlight in four colors and attach notes
  • Export highlights to Markdown — Obsidian, Notion, or any plain-text vault
  • Reading statistics: time today, this week, all time, and your streak
  • Import from Files — iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox
  • Everything stays on your device
